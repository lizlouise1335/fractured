About

The story behind Fractured Research

What is this?

Fractured Research is a personal platform for publishing and exploring research articles created in collaboration with artificial intelligence. Each piece represents an experiment — a conversation between human curiosity and machine reasoning.

Philosophy

The name "Fractured" reflects the nature of AI-assisted research: ideas that break apart conventional thinking, recombine in unexpected ways, and illuminate previously unseen connections. These articles don't claim to be definitive — they are explorations, starting points, and provocations.

How it works

Articles are drafted through dialogue with AI systems, then refined and curated for clarity and insight. The Compose tool lets you write new articles with full Markdown support. All articles are stored locally in your browser and can be managed from the Articles page.

Built with care. No frameworks. No trackers. Just words and ideas.
